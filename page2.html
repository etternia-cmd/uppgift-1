Overview of Different Rhythm Games

Rhythm games come in various forms and styles, each offering a unique experience for players. Some of them require you to play actual instruments, while others focus on button-mashing or timing-based challenges. I will now provide an example of some of the rhythm games that you read about on the Main Page:

  • osu! (2007)

    A popular rhythm game that challenges players to click circles, slide sliders, and spin spinners in time with the music. Inspired by the original nintendo DS game Osu! Tatakae Ouendan, osu! is a PC port created by Dean Herbert. What made the game stand out was its emphasis on community-created content and the ability for players to customize their gameplay experience such as making custom maps, skins and making the interface of the game more personalized which the original game didnt allow.

  • Stepmania (2001)

    A free and open-source rhythm game that is a PC port of Dance Dance Revolution. Instead of using your feet and stepping on arrows to the beat, you can now use a keyboard instead! Not only that, but it also allowed for custom maps, skins and tons of optimization, making the game extremely personalized for every player.

  • Etterna (2013)

    The open-source successor to Stepmania (people refer to it as Stepmania 7 in some cases), focused more on competitive play. It features advanced scoring systems and most importantly, a global leaderboard both for players and maps, where people can compete and compare against eachother in a cutthroat fashion. It's popular among players looking for a more challenging experience.

  • Friday Night Funkin' (2020)

    A VSRG (Vertical Scrolling Rhythm Game) that gained popularity for its catchy music and unique art style. Players must hit notes in time with the music to progress through the story. (The same way any other stepmania game is played). Unlike the other VSRGs mentioned above, this game doesn't have a strong focus on competitive play and its mostly made for casual players.

  • Beat Saber (2018)

    A VR (Virtual Reality)rhythm game where players use lightsabers to slash blocks representing musical beats. It combines physical movement with rhythm-based gameplay for an immersive experience. Extremely unique and a never-before-seen concept for a rhythm game, it also featured custom map creation and a vibrant community.
