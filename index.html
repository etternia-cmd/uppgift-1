What is a rhythm game?

A rhythm game is a music-themed action video game that challenges a player's sense of rhythm. Players must press buttons in a sequence that matches the rhythm of a song, often in time with visual cues on the screen. The goal is to achieve a high score by hitting the notes accurately. They have been around since the early 1980s, but where they really took off was around the late 1990s and early 2000s. Some of the popular games around that period were the first dance dance revolution games (DDR for short), guitar hero 1, rock band and so on.

The most popular and widely recognized rhythm is Dance Dance Revolution (DDR). Not only was an arcade stable game where you could dance to the rhythm of song, but it also sparked the beginning of what we call, a VSRG (Vertical Scrolling Rhythm game) basically meaning that there are objects falling from the sky (or from the bottom of the screen in some cases) and you have to press them notes accurately when the object approaches the target.
